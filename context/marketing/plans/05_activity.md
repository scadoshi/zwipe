# 5. Activity

**Status:** Planned.

**Theme:** How you actually pick: a year of swipes as a heatmap, your totals, what you keep and what you skip.

## Soundtrack

Driving and rhythmic: punchy percussion, pulsing synth-orchestral bass, a steady build, about 125 bpm.

## Script

Callum, the mix delivery (see README). Hook on screen: *What do your Magic: The Gathering swipes say about you?*

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | [dramatically] What do your Magic swipes say about you? |
| 2 | A whole year of swiping, day by day. |
| 3 | Every add, every skip, counted. |
| 4 | The roles and tags you keep picking. |
| 5 | [serious] And the cards you can't stand. |
| End card | Zwipe. The Magic: The Gathering deck builder. Free, no ads, on iOS, Android and web. |

## Pacing

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Activity opens, the heatmap draws in |
| 3-6s | Tap a busy day on the heatmap |
| 6-9s | Totals |
| 9-13s | Card roles and Oracle tags rate charts over a Show pick |
| 13-16s | Most skipped cards |
| 16-23s | End card, closing line |
