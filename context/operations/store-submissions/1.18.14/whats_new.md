# 1.18.14 release notes

One text, both stores. 481 characters, 19 to spare.

```
- Sort Decks by name, size, format, price or last changed
- Card details slide in from the right with prices; printings show as a cover flow
- Card rows show colors, price and P/T; commander and MVP names sit in chips
- An Archidekt import fills in an empty deck's format and commander
- Partner search offers only partners your commander can pair with
- Swipes confirm in a quiet chip, with a setting to change it
- Logging out ends only that device's session
- Many smaller fixes
```

The 89 entries from 1.18.9 to 1.18.14 in `zwipe-core/src/content/changelog/mod.rs` run to several thousand characters, so this text keeps what a phone user can point at. Sort on Decks, the card details panel, the cover flow, the card rows and the commander and MVP chips stand for the deck and card screens; the Archidekt fill, partner search and swipe confirmations are the new behavior; per-device logout is the one session change a user notices. The web app, zwipe.net, the keyboard and screen reader work, the wording and hint passes and the light theme fixes fall into the last line; the in-app changelog carries all of them.
