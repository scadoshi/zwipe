# 1.11.3 release notes

One text, both stores. 452 characters, 48 to spare.

```
- Home counters show the last numbers fetched when offline, with no error when they cannot refresh
- Messages show two lines until tapped, then the whole text wrapped to fit the screen
- Requests give up after thirty seconds instead of waiting forever
- Changing your email no longer writes the new address to the device log
- Android: the sign-in session is left out of device backups
- Too many wrong passwords lock out only the device that sent them
```

The eight 1.11.3 entries from `zwipe-core/src/content/changelog/mod.rs`, folded to six so they clear Play's cap: the two home counter entries became one, and the two message entries became one. Nothing was dropped. Phrases, no period at the end, no examples.
