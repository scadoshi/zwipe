# 6. Know every card

**Status:** Planned.

**Theme:** Everything about a card without leaving the deck: rules, both faces, every printing, prices.

## Soundtrack

Mysterious, a little arcane: low strings, harp runs, soft choir pads (no words), building curiosity, about 100 bpm. The library's creepy track can stand in.

## Script

Callum, the mix delivery (see README). Hook on screen: *Know every card in your Magic: The Gathering deck*

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | [dramatically] Know every card in your Magic deck. |
| 2 | Rulings, one tap away. |
| 3 | Flip a two-faced card. |
| 4 | Every printing, one swipe at a time. |
| 5 | [serious] And what it costs today. |
| End card | Zwipe. The Magic: The Gathering deck builder. Free, no ads, on iOS, Android and web. |

## Pacing

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Card details open from a deck row |
| 3-6s | The rules dialog |
| 6-9s | A double-faced card flips |
| 9-13s | The printing sheet's cover flow, three printings |
| 13-16s | Prices on the chosen printing |
| 16-23s | End card, closing line |
