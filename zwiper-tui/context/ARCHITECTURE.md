# zwiper-tui architecture

Every screen lives in its own folder under `src/lib/inbound/screens/`, and the rest of the crate reaches it through one match arm per screen in `screens/mod.rs`. Two people adding two screens touch the same file only on those arm lines.

```
src/lib/inbound/
├── app/            App (the back stack plus Shared), Context, Effects, Navigation
├── nav/            Nav, the back stack
├── toward.rs       Toward, which way a focus moves: next or previous
├── event_loop/     the loop: keys, results, settling navigation, spawning requests
│   └── harness.rs  test-only Harness that drives screens with no network
├── screens/
│   ├── mod.rs      Screen, Request, Update, and the arms that route to each screen
│   ├── login/      mod.rs, keys.rs, draw.rs, tests.rs
│   ├── register/  forgot_password/  update_required/
│   ├── home/  activity/  playstyle/  commander_maybeboard/  commander_swipe/
│   ├── oracle_tags/  otag_examples/  printings/  image_preview/
│   ├── decks/  deck/  deck_form/  deck_import/  deck_export/
│   ├── swipe/  deck_cards/  remove_cards/  card_sheets/
│   └── account/  config/  privacy/  changelog/
└── widgets/        shared drawing pieces, each with its own tests
```

## Domain

What a screen shows and decides comes from zwipe-ui, the crate zwiper, zwiper-web and zwiper-tui share: card faces, the changelog, the account forms, Activity, the color map, Playstyle, home's figures, buy links, the deck list, form, export and warning fixes, the card stack, the card stores and catalog, the filter's sections, pickers and draft, Add's rules, the deck cards pass, the land and budget crossings, hint copy, toasts, timings, session rules, generation tickets and the problem report link. Wherever zwipe-ui settles a difference between the TUI and the app, the TUI follows it. The count-up, logo decode, grid rain and splash timeline come from orangui-core.

`src/lib/domain/` keeps only what the terminal alone needs: `theme` (the palettes, generated by `scripts/gen_themes.py` and `gen_theme_extras.py`), `legal` (the privacy policy's HTML as text) and `decode` (the logo's decode frames from orangui-core, timed in whole 40 ms ticks).

Screens act on zwipe-ui's answers instead of copying its rules. The filter sheet drives `zwipe_ui::screens::card_filter` and shows the `Toast` each action returns with `cx.show(toast)`. A screen that can see replies out of order keeps a `zwipe_ui::generation::Generation`, sends each request with a `Ticket` from it, and lands a reply only while `is_current(ticket)` holds.

## Screens

- Sign-in: `login`, `register` and `forgot_password` lay out through `login::draw::frame`: the hero over the form, its one button a row under the last field (`Log in`, `Create profile`, `Send reset link`), the whole group placed as one. Each keeps its focus as an `Option`: Esc in the form ends typing and leaves no field active, Tab or Shift+Tab goes back in, and with no field active the letters are commands (`q` quits and `!` opens Help everywhere; Login adds `c` Create profile and `f` Forgot password). Esc never quits: with no field active it does nothing on Login and goes back to Login from the other two. The key bar says which state the screen is in: `Tab move  Enter …  F1 help  Esc done` in the form, with Enter named for what it does from the focused stop, and `Tab fields  …  F1 help  q quit` (`Esc back` before `q quit` on Register and Reset password) once typing has ended. Beside them sits `update_required`, the notice the app draws over every screen while the server refuses this version. It is not a `Screen`: while it shows, the event loop quits on Esc or `q` and drops every other key.
- Home and what it leads to: `home` (counters, color map, featured card; on a terminal with kitty, iTerm2 or sixel images the featured card's image loads through Add's Scryfall-only fetch into `shared.caches.flavor_image`, one image under its URL, and shows beside the quote on a wide panel or above it on a tall narrow one, the panel at least 18 rows; half blocks, `ZWIPER_TUI_IMAGES=off`, a small panel or a card with no image keep the text alone), `activity` (`v`), `playstyle` (`p`, the color map's Playstyle), `commander_maybeboard`, and `commander_swipe`, the `SwipeSelect` overlay in its Suggested and Commander modes.
- Oracle tags: `oracle_tags`, the dictionary, whose Use leaves a slug in `shared.caches.handoff.oracle_tag` for the screen under it; `otag_examples`, a tag's example cards; `printings`, a card's printings to pick or browse; and `image_preview`.
- Decks: `decks` (its More sheet opens the Commander maybeboard and Playstyle), `deck`, `deck_form` (Create and Edit; `open_create_with_commander` seeds Create from Playstyle, the commander swipe and the maybeboard), `deck_import`, `deck_export`.
- Cards: `swipe` (Add), `deck_cards`, `remove_cards`, and `card_sheets`, which holds the filter, details and printing sheets those screens and the commander screens open over themselves.
- `account`, `config`, `privacy`, `changelog`.

A sheet that opens a screen over its host, as the filter sheet's Dictionary does, gets its answer back through the host's arm of `Screen::on_return`: `card_sheets::filter::returned_from_dictionary(sheet, cx)` adopts the slug and says whether the return was the dictionary's, so the host skips its own refresh.

## How a key or a result flows

A key goes to `Screen::handle_key` on the screen at the top of the stack, which calls that screen's `keys::handle(view, code, cx)`. The handler changes its own state directly, or, on a screen switched to its zwipe-ui model, hands an action to the screen's `dispatch` (see below). Anything else it asks for through the `Context`: `cx.request(..)` queues background work, `cx.push`, `cx.back`, `cx.replace` and `cx.reset` queue navigation, `cx.quit()` ends the app, `cx.toast(..)` shows a toast, and `cx.shared` holds the theme, the user, the status line and the card-image picker. Handlers never touch the network or the stack themselves.

When the handler returns, `EventLoop::settle` applies the navigation in order. Going back pops the top screen and calls `Screen::on_return` on the one uncovered, which is how the deck view reloads after swiping and the deck list reloads after a deck closes. Back from the bottom screen quits.

Each queued `Request` runs on a tokio task through `screens::perform`, which hands a screen's own requests to that screen's `perform(account, request)`. The answer comes back as an `Update`. `screens::apply` routes a screen's update to its `apply(nav, update, cx)`, which finds its own screen anywhere in the stack with `nav.find_mut(pick)`, so a result still lands when another screen is open over it. A screen decides for itself what an update means when its screen is closed; config's preferences, for one, still set the theme.

The app's own work sits in the same enums as plain variants: `CheckVersion`, `SignOut`, `RememberTheme`, `MarkHintShown`, and the `Upkept`, `SessionEnded`, `SignedOut`, `Status`, `Toast` and `HintMarked` updates.

## Switching a screen to its zwipe-ui model

Every screen with a model in `zwipe_ui::screens` drives it instead of keeping its own rules. `forgot_password`, `register` and `login` are the worked examples; read `zwipe-ui/context/screen-models.md` first.

- The view holds `pub(crate) model: State` (imported as `use zwipe_ui::screens::<screen>::{Action, Destination, Effect, State}`) plus only what the terminal alone keeps: the `TextInput` boxes, the focus, the hero, and the `Instant`s animations run from. Its `Default` starts the model with `State::new()`.
- One function in `mod.rs`, `dispatch(view, action, cx)`, is the only caller of `model.update`. It applies the action, copies the model's views into the widgets that store them (`email.set_error(model.error())`, the focus when the model ends the form); effects that rewrite a box the client owns (`SetIdentifier`, `ClearPassword`) call `set_value` on it, then performs each `Effect` in order in one `match`: a request effect becomes `cx.request(Request::.. { ticket, .. }.into())` and starts its spinner clock, `Navigate(to)` maps every `Destination` to `cx.back`, `cx.push`, `cx.reset` or an `open` in one arm each, `Toast(toast)` is `cx.show(toast)`, and a hint is `hint::once(cx, key)`.
- Keys turn into actions and go through `dispatch`. A text box still takes its own keystroke first (`view.email.handle_key(code)`), then its whole value goes to the model as the field's edit action. Keys the model has no action for (focus, Tab, `q`, `!`, Esc that only ends typing) stay in `keys.rs` as before.
- A ticket rides out on the screen's `Request` variant and back on its `Update` (`Request::Send { ticket, email }`, `Update::Sent { ticket }`, `Update::Failed { ticket, error }`). `apply` turns the update into the model's reply action and, when the screen is still in the stack, dispatches it; a failure's error toast goes up through `cx.toast` before that, so it shows even when the screen has closed, as the app's request runner does.
- `dispatch` has no `Nav`, so an effect that hands something to another screen (Register's `Prefill`) leaves it in `cx.shared.caches.handoff` and the receiving screen takes it in its arm of `Screen::on_return`, as the app's root-level signals do (`handoff.login_prefill`, taken by `login::on_return`). Funnel events (`Request::Record`) go out where `dispatch` performs the request effect they belong to.
- An effect that the TUI already does elsewhere as part of a larger step gets that step split, not copied: Login's `SignedIn` is `login::adopt` (theme, user) and `Navigate(Home)` is `home::open`, while a launch with a saved session still calls `login::signed_in`, which runs both.
- Draw reads the model's accessors (`is_loading()`, `is_sent()`, `error()`) and its copy consts (`EMAIL`, `SEND`, `SENT_TITLE`) and never decides anything. `Screen::animating` asks the model whether to spin (`view.model.is_loading()`).
- Delete what the model now owns: the TUI's error strings and copy consts, `touched`/`attempted` flags, validation and gate functions, `sending: bool` or `Option<Instant>` used as a loading flag, and any `Generation` the view kept. Spinner clocks stay as a plain `Instant` set when the request effect is performed.
- Tests: delete the ones that only checked a rule the model now tests (which message, which gate); keep every key, focus, drawing and key bar test, reading state through `view.model`. Requests in `pending()` now carry tickets, so take them from the queue (`last_ticket(&harness)`) after a real submit, never set the view's loading by hand; `Ticket` has no public constructor, and `Generation::default().next_ticket()` gives one only where any ticket will do (a `Debug` test). Add one end-to-end wiring test per fetch that a stale or repeated reply changes nothing.
- A screen that opens with `Load` builds its view, dispatches `Load` on it, then pushes it (`oracle_tags::open`): `cx.push` and hints are queued, so the effects can run before the view is on the stack. A model that reads an app-wide cache it does not own (the dictionary's `Catalog<'_>`) gets one function that maps the cache's states into the model's, and draw passes it to the model's views. A hand-off the app performs through a host callback while the overlay stays up (`AdoptTag`) becomes the handoff plus `cx.back()`, since a TUI host adopts in `Screen::on_return`.
- A request effect that carries the query the model built (`SearchCards { ticket, query }`) rides out as is on the screen's `Request` (`otag_examples::Request::Page { ticket, query }`), so the TUI's own query builder goes; the `Request` drops `Eq`, since `CardQuery` has only `PartialEq`. A test fixture that opens the screen takes the opening request's ticket from the queue before `harness.clear()` and hands it back with the harness. A client animation the model leaves to the client (the card leaving on Next) is started in the view before it dispatches, while the card is still current.
- State an effect hands to the next screen ahead of `Navigate` (`SeedCommander(card)`, then `Navigate(CreateDeck)`) is held in a local inside `dispatch` and taken by the `Navigate` arm (`deck_form::open_create_with_commander`), since both arrive in one `update`. A text field whose edit action takes a new ticket on every send (the maybeboard's `Type`, which restarts the debounce) is sent only when the box's value changed, not on cursor moves. One `Update::Failed { ticket, message: Option<String> }` covers every request of a screen whose model answers all failures with one `Failed { ticket }`: `Some` raises the error toast first, `None` is a quiet search. A count the model reads but does not show, which a TUI-only screen needs too (the commander swipe's `DeckLimit` takes the deck count), stays in the view beside the model and is written in `apply` before dispatching.
- A screen opened for one item opens on its id (`deck::open(cx, deck_id)`, `deck_export::open(cx, deck_id)`) and draws from the model's replies, never from a value the screen under it handed over. Tests answer its fetches from the queue: the deck view's `serve(harness, deck)` lands the latest profile and deck fetches, and `Harness::on_top` runs a dispatch no key reaches, such as a second `Load`.
- A reply can land while another screen covers its own. When an effect would move the stack (the deck view's `Navigate` after a clone or a delete, the deck form's after a save), `apply` checks `nav.top()` and performs everything else, so the model still settles but the screen on top stays; the deck form does it through `run(view, action, cx, on_top)`, which `dispatch` calls with `true`.
- An effect the terminal has no use for is matched and dropped with a comment saying why (the deck view's `FetchCommander`, since it draws no commander art), never left to a catch-all arm.
- A value another screen hands back that the model sets only through a toggle (the commander swipe's Filter identity on Playstyle) goes in as the action that reaches it from the current value (`ShowAll`, or `ToggleIdentity` when it differs), before the reply action (`SwipeClosed`). A TUI behavior the model has no action for (a printing picked on a Playstyle staple row) stays in the view as an overlay on the model's view (`staple_printings`, applied in `matching_staples`), reset when a load the view sent lands, and is reported as a model gap. Tests reach a dispatch no key sends through a small `act(harness, action)` over `Harness::on_top`.
- An effect the client answers at once with an action (Add's `TakeUndo` with `Taken`, `Rerun` with `Filter` holding the client's filter) is answered through a queue inside `dispatch`: the performer returns the answering action, and `dispatch` feeds it to the model after the rest of the batch, so `update` is never re-entered. Entrance marks the model sets and clears (`Enter`, `StopEnter`) live on the view per pile, and the key that undoes starts the entrance motion after `dispatch` when its pile is marked. A screen that used to open on a value now opens on its id and its model fetches (`swipe::open(cx, deck_id)` sends `Request::Deck`); draw keeps ghosting while that first fetch is out (`Pile::Empty` before `is_deck_loaded()`), and tests answer it with `Harness::serve_deck` before `serve`.
- A model's `Effect::Wait(ticket)` is a request like any other: `perform` sleeps the model's tick (`account::TICK`) and answers with an update carrying the ticket, which `apply` hands back as `Action::Tick`. Every reply redraws, so a countdown needs no arm in `Screen::animating`.
- The TUI's tasks outlive the screen that sent them (the app's die with it), so a reply can arrive after its screen has closed and opened again. Tickets are unique across the process (see `zwipe-ui/context/screen-models.md`, "Loading and generations"), so the reopened model never accepts the earlier visit's ticket and `apply` can dispatch any reply into whichever view is open. Toasts and the shared user still land whether or not the screen that asked is open.
- A screen whose data outlives a visit (Home) builds the model's `Cached` from the TUI's caches in one `snapshot(shared)` and sends `Load` on every mount; `apply` writes each reply to the cache whichever visit asked, then dispatches it into the open view, whose model drops a ticket it did not issue. A fetch the TUI already runs app-wide (Home's `FetchCounts`, which Login's hero shares through `shared.counts`) keeps the model's ticket on the view and hands it back when the shared update lands (`home::counts_arrived`). A clock-driven action the app sends on its session refresh (`CheckFlavor { now }`) goes out on the `Upkept` update.
- A model that owns the text its boxes show (the deck form's name, price and command-zone inputs) gets a `sync` step at the end of `dispatch`: each box takes the model's text only when it differs, so the cursor stays put while typing and a load, pick or knock-on clear still lands in the box. A view that resets with a model view (a results list's highlighted row) snapshots that view before `update` and compares after.
- A request effect that carries a zwipe-core contract without `PartialEq` (`HttpCreateDeckProfile`) rides out in `deck_form::Body<T>`, which compares by the JSON it sends, so the screen's `Request` keeps `PartialEq` for the harness.
- A swipe overlay over a host's list (the commander swipe on `swipe_select`) keeps what the host handed it unnarrowed (`held`) and hands the model the pile as dealt: a client-side filter narrows it and sends `Redeal`, so the model keeps the swiped cards behind the top one. The exit motion starts in the view before `Skip` or `Save` while the card is still current; the entrance is the model's `Enter(side)`, which wins over the side the card left. `Pick` and `Navigate(Previous)` both close through the view's `close`, which leaves the host its handoff.
- A model whose `Load` starts the screen over (the deck cards view's) is sent `Load` again only where the app would mount the screen afresh: on the return from a screen its own `Navigate` opened (Add, Remove), marked on the view as it goes, never on the return from an overlay the app keeps inside the screen (oracle tag examples). An effect the client answers at once (`Refilter` with `Filter` holding the client's filter, a `Navigate` away with `Leave`, which writes the waiting bursts) queues its action inside `dispatch`, which feeds it to the model after the batch. A fetch the app answers in two steps (`ProfileLoaded` partway through `FetchDeck`, then `Loaded`) comes back as one `Update` that `apply` dispatches as both, in order. A view the TUI keeps from the model's views (the deck cards `display` under the client's filter) is taken at the end of every `dispatch`, and the cursor clamped to it there.
- A model whose undo answers every failure with one `Failed { ticket }` while the app tells two apart (Remove's session that could not refresh stops the entrance, a failed call toasts "Failed to undo") gets its own `Update::UndoFailed { ticket, failure }`, which `apply` settles on the view before it dispatches `Failed`. The side an undone card came back from (`Effect::Enter`) lives on the view as `entering`, coloring the top card's border until the next key.
- A sheet a host opens over itself (the card filter, `card_sheets::filter::FilterSheet`) keeps its model inside the sheet, built fresh on each opening, so `Option<FilterSheet>` on the host is still what says it is up. Its `dispatch` returns what the host acts on (`SheetOutcome::Applied(filter)` from `Apply` then `Close`) instead of reaching into the host. Controls the model has no action for (chips, steppers, typing) edit a clone of `model.draft()` and hand it back with `Edit`; the model's `Restore` is dropped, since the sheet never edits the host's filter in place. Whether the dictionary is up stays a flag on the sheet (`in_dictionary`), as the app's overlay signal, and the host's `on_return` hands it the slug as `UseTag`. A host that resets through its own model, as the app's `on_clear` does (Add's `ResetFilter(draft)`), takes `r` before the sheet (`filter::keys::resets`) and runs its model with its `SetFilter` writing the sheet's draft (`swipe::reset_filter`, `FilterSink::Sheet`), never the applied filter. Hosts the app gives a quiet reset (the commander swipe, the deck form's swipe picker, the Commander maybeboard) open the sheet with `open_quiet`. An app-wide cache that fails while the sheet is up (a catalog list) tells the sheet on top from the cache's own `apply` (`ListsFailed`), as the app's reactive check does.
- Pitfalls: `TextInput::handle_key` clears the box's error on every edit, so `dispatch` must set it back from the model after each action, not only on submit. Replies reach a view through `nav.find_mut(pick)`, so a toast that must outlive the screen goes up before that lookup; a success that lands after the screen closed has no model to tell it what to do and does nothing, which is also what the app does, since its task dies with the screen. A text field's edit action takes the box's whole value, never the key, so cursor moves (Left, Home) are sent too; the model treats a repeat as a no-op.

## Crashes and error reports

The terminal client reports as `ClientPlatform::Terminal` (`"terminal"`) on sign-in, crash reports and usage batches, which needs a zerver that knows the value.

- `outbound/crash_store.rs`: `run` installs a panic hook before the terminal starts, so ratatui's own hook wraps it and restores the terminal first. The first panic of a process writes `crash.json` to the config folder. The next launch's `Request::SendCrashReport` posts it with `record_crash`, unauthenticated, and deletes it only when the server took it.
- `outbound/usage_buffer.rs`: the app's `report_error`, keyed and capped the same way. `screens::perform` runs every request inside a task-local naming its screen (the screen's module) and action (its Request's `action()`, a fixed snake-case name per variant, so renaming a variant keeps its reports). `screens::shown(&error)`, which `screens::failure` calls, keeps the error under that name and returns the message to show. A screen shows a `ClientError` through one of the two, never `to_user_message` directly. `Request::FlushUsage` sends what waits as one usage batch every 30 seconds, as the app does, with swipes and searches at zero; a clean quit sends once more after the terminal is restored, giving up after 2 seconds; signing out drops it.
- A debug build with `ZWIPER_TUI_DEBUG_CRASH` set panics on F12, to check the crash report and the terminal restore by hand. Release builds have no such key.

## Adding a screen

Say the screen is `search`.

1. Make `screens/search/` with `mod.rs`, `keys.rs`, `draw.rs` and `tests.rs`. Copy `decks/` as a starting point: it is the smallest screen with background work.
2. In `search/mod.rs`, define the state (`SearchView`), and if it does background work, a `Request` and an `Update` enum with a `From` impl for each into `screens::Request` and `screens::Update`. Add `open(cx, ..)` that pushes the screen and queues its first requests, `perform(account, request)`, and `apply(nav, update, cx)` with a private `pick` that matches `Screen::Search`.
3. In `search/keys.rs`, write `handle(view, code, cx)`. Esc is usually `cx.back()`.
4. In `search/draw.rs`, write `draw(f, shared, view)`. Use `shared.palette` for every style.
5. Register it in `screens/mod.rs`, one line each:
   - `pub(crate) mod search;`
   - a `Screen::Search(Box<SearchView>)` variant
   - an arm in `Screen::handle_key` and one in `Screen::draw`
   - a `Request::Search(search::Request)` variant and its arm in `perform`
   - an `Update::Search(search::Update)` variant and its arm in `apply`
   - an arm in `Screen::animating` only if something on it moves, and in `Screen::on_return` only if it should refresh when a screen over it closes
6. Call `search::open(cx)` from the screen that leads to it.

A screen with no background work, like `privacy`, skips the request and update enums and their arms.

Tests go through `Harness`: `Harness::on(Screen::Search(..))` or `harness.open(search::open)`, then `press`, `type_text`, `apply`, and assertions on `pending()` and `screen()`. `test_support::screen_text(&harness.app)` renders the app at 100 by 40 for drawing tests. `test_support::assert_key_bar_fits(&harness.app, "Esc back")` draws at 80 by 24 and 60 by 20 and checks the key bar's last hint shows whole.

## Screen chrome

No screen has a title row: content starts on the first row, and the key bar along the bottom names the keys, Help & feedback and the screen's hint key (see `action_bar` below).

`App::draw_at` fills the frame with the theme's background, then draws the grid settled (`grid::draw(f, palette, area, None)`) behind every screen, the update notice included. The grid only sets backgrounds. Boxes paint their own background over it so they sit on it like cards: everything framed with `panel::bordered`, which covers panels, fields, text areas, sheets, dialogs, alerts, toasts and popups. Loose content (screen text, key bars, buttons, the logo, chips' surroundings, titles outside boxes) draws with no background of its own, so the tiles show up to its letters: style it with `palette.text()` or another fg-only style, never `palette.base` (which sets the background) or a `Clear`. A reversed chip may keep `palette.base.reversed()`.

A border glyph sits in the middle of its cell, so a box's filled border cells would leave a plain half-cell band between the tiles and its line. Once the screen, the dialogs and the toasts have drawn, `grid::reach_edges` gives every cell that holds the plain background and touches a showing tile its own tile back: the outer border and any label on it sit on the grid, the inside stays solid, and a box drawn over another box keeps its border on that box. Reversed cells keep their fill. A palette with no colors of its own draws no tiles. The splash's dissolve and the theme wipe both keep tiles in place: a cell the splash holds shows its settled tile, and a row the wipe has not reached shows the old theme's tile.

## Widgets

All live in `widgets/` and draw in the account theme's `Palette`.

- `toast`: success, error, info and warning toasts in the top-left corner, at most three, the newest on top and the older ones tucked behind it; Ctrl+T expands them. The kinds and the 3, 5 and 8 s bands are zwipe-ui's. `cx.toast(kind, message)` shows one for `toast::NORMAL` (5 s), `cx.toast_for(kind, message, toast::QUICK | NORMAL | LONG)` for 3, 5 or 8 s, `cx.show(toast)` shows a `zwipe_ui::toast::Toast` for its own band, and the `Toast` update does the same from a result.
- `alert`: `AlertDialog` with a title, rules, a body and buttons, one focused; a button can be danger or disabled, and `draw_with` leaves room under the body for a field. Its buttons are `button`'s bracketed row on one line, or one per line when the row is wider than the dialog (Help's three on any screen, since a dialog is at most 56 cells wide).
- `button`: every button, drawn as its label in brackets on one line, `[ Log in ]`, at every size. `Button::new(label)` is plain: brackets in the muted text color (brighter than the border) around a label in the text color. `.danger()` takes the error color for the label (Log out, Delete), and `.disabled(bool)` mutes it. A focused button draws its brackets and a bold label in its focus color (the accent for a plain one, its own color for a danger one); no button fills its background, so focus is the only highlight and a filled block is always a tag. `button::draw_row(f, palette, area, row, focused)` draws a row centered on one line with two cells between buttons, `button::line` returns that line, `button::row_width` measures it, and `button::spans` is one button for a line of text, such as Account's and Config's row buttons. The sign-in screens pass their row to `login::draw::frame`, which puts it on the line under the form, with a row between them when the hero loses nothing to it, and places Register's group a row down from the top when there is room so the logo never sits on the screen's edge. Account keeps Log out and More on one line over its key bar, and its Change sheets have only Save changes, with `Esc back` in their key bar; Config, Privacy and Changelog have no button row, since `Esc back` in the key bar is their way out. Every `?` that opens an About topic on screen is drawn by `button::about(palette, focused)` as `[?]`, with no inner spaces since it is one glyph: at rest brackets and `?` are muted at every site, and it takes the bold accent look while it is the marker the screen's About key opens right now (the focused row on Account and Config, the focused field on Register's and the Change sheet's password, the focused section or group on Activity and Playstyle, the picked column on the Oracle tags table head, the Oracle tags section in the filter sheet, the shown section tab of the focused panel in the deck view, the focused field on the deck form while it takes no typing, since its `i` opens it). `button::ABOUT_WIDTH` is its 3 cells and `button::about_corner` pads it a space each side for a box's top-right corner (Home's Color map, a sheet with a hint). It sits one space after its label, and a text box's label takes it through `text_input::Label::About`. Key bar hints such as `? hint`, `? About` and `i about` are keys, not markers, and stay bare.
- `sheet`: a bottom sheet with a scrolling body, a focused line kept in view, an optional `[?]` corner and footer keys; body lines too wide wrap between words under their own indent. `sheet::frame` draws only the chrome, for a sheet with a form inside.
- `hint`: `?` topics and one-time hints. The copy is zwipe-ui's typed spans (`Block`, `Span`, `Role`); this widget draws a bullet in the muted color, a key chip reversed in its role's palette color and an emphasized word bold in it. `hint::topic(cx, HintTopic::Email)` opens a topic; `hint::once(cx, key)` opens the app's one-time hint for a key from `zwipe_core::domain::user::hints` the first time only, marks it and sends `mark_hint_shown`; `hint::reopen(cx, key)` opens it again for the key bar's `? hint`; `zwipe_ui::hint::one_time::swipe_select(mode)` with `hint::once_with` covers the one hint whose copy changes. While a hint is up it takes every key.
- `hero`: the logo and everybody's three counters, each in its own tile, on the sign-in screens, over the shared `Counts` copy. `hero::mount(cx)` makes one and fetches the counts when they are stale. Home reads the same `shared.counts` for its Everybody figures and asks for `Request::FetchCounts` under the same 60 second rule, so Login and Home never fetch twice; the user's own counters live in `shared.caches`.
- `figures`: home's, Login's and Activity's strip of three rolling figures. `figures::tiles` boxes each figure in its own rounded tile, number over label, as the app's `StatsStrip`; home and Login use it, falling back to the plain strip on a terminal under 47 columns, and home also under 22 rows.
- `color_map`: the color map's tiles two cells wide in their four shades, the ring sweep, the loading ghost, and the key with its chips and Playstyle button. A map too big for its room draws at half scale, one cell wide and half a row tall a tile in half blocks, and crops around its middle only if even that does not fit; the model and its golden test are zwipe-ui's and stay as they are.
- `stagger`: entrances inside a screen, delayed and rising, and the hero's scope-in.
- `card_rows`: expandable card rows with classification, printings (`printings::open` in `Mode::Pick`) and image keys, for Activity, Playstyle and the maybeboard.
- `otag`: an oracle tag's definition with its `x Examples` key, and `RoleTags`, a row of tag chips with one open. The details sheet and the deck cards view's open row reveal a card's tags with it.
- `item_list`: a list of items of different heights, one focused, paged to keep it in view; a focused item taller than the list keeps its last line, a card row's keys, in its bottom rows, as Activity's `draw_lines` does for its column.
- `card_text` and `card_info`: a card as text, and the card screens' info block, details body and text stand-in.
- `menu` and `pill`: a sheet of buttons, and one chip drawn on its own.
- `text_area`: a text box of many lines, for pasting a decklist, with the same centered label as `text_input` and nothing inside while empty. Import's `?` hint (`deck_import::hint_content`, the app's copy plus an example list) teaches the paste format instead.
- Animations, each driven by elapsed time with a matching `animating` check for `Screen::animating`: `logo` (the decode, `logo::draw(f, area, art, palette.logo, Some(elapsed))`), `count_up` (`CountUp::new(start, value)`, `set`, `shown(now)`, its frames from orangui-core), `grid` (orangui-core's rain, `grid::draw(f, palette, area, Some(elapsed))`, which the splash runs; see Screen chrome for the settled grid), `entrance` (`entrance::draw(f, area, elapsed)`; the app runs it on every new screen), `spinner` (a request in flight), `splash` (the opening over the first screen) and `wipe` (the sweep between themes); the app runs the last two itself. Logos draw in `palette.logo`.
- `card_motion`: a swiped card flying off and an undone one coming back, after the app's `card-stack-exit-*` (0.18 s) and `card-stack-enter-*` (0.2 s), both on CSS `ease-out`. The card slides off in the swipe direction, clipped at the bounds, and narrows toward an edge-on sliver (its width for left and right, its height for up) to stand in for the tilt: it gives up blank and border-only columns first, then clips its text from the trailing end, so words are cut, never thinned. For the second half it fades to a dim outline, so the card underneath shows whole. Down is undo and has no exit. Add, Remove, the deck form's swipe picker, the commander swipe and the oracle tag Examples use it. A swipe screen adopts it in four steps:
  1. Keep `motion: Option<CardMotion<T>>` on the view. `T` is whatever draws the card that left; `card_motion::Snapshot { card, face }` suits a card screen.
  2. On a committed swipe, before the stack moves on, set `CardMotion::exit(direction, snapshot, Instant::now())`. On an undo that brings a card back, set `CardMotion::enter(side_it_left, Instant::now())`, or the side the screen's model names (the commander swipe's `Enter(Left)`). A new swipe replaces the motion; keys act on the stack at once.
  3. In `draw`, keep only a motion that is `running(now)`. Draw the resting card first: the new top card during an exit, the card under the top one during an entrance. Then take `motion.placement(now, rest, bounds)` for the moving card's resting rect and call `card_motion::draw(f.buffer_mut(), &placement, palette.base, |buf| widget.render(rest, buf))`. An exit draws its snapshot; an entrance draws the stack's own top card.
  4. Report `running(Instant::now())` from the screen's arm in `Screen::animating`.

  `card_motion::side_style` gives a direction's color. Add holds Sixel and iTerm2 images back while a card moves, since those protocols draw as one escape that later cells skip; half blocks and kitty placeholders are ordinary cells and stay.
- `text_input`: a one-line box with cursor movement, masking and an error line, its label centered on the top border. No box shows placeholder text: an empty box draws empty. The caller places the terminal cursor with `cursor_position`.
- `collapsible`: a section header marking it open or closed, and an accordion that keeps one open, for Activity and Playstyle.
- `info`: the "?" popup of bullet points.
- `action_bar`: the bottom row of key hints. A swipe screen builds its swipe keys with `action_bar::swipes(palette, right, left, up, down)`, which colors each verb like the app's swipe hints (right success, left error, up warning, down tertiary), and draws them with `styled_line`. The labels use only add, remove, skip, maybeboard and undo (the commander swipe and the oracle tag Examples too: Examples reads skip and undo); toasts and hint prose stay the app's own words, so Remove's skip still toasts "Kept". A bar too wide for the screen wraps between hints, never splitting a key from its action: a screen reserves `action_bar::height(&bar, width)` rows for it and draws `action_bar::wrap(bar, width)`, and the deck view wraps its pane keys and screen keys each on their own. Keys inside text a paragraph wraps, like a card row's actions, go through `action_bar::unbreakable` so the wrap falls only between hints. Every screen but the sign-in screens passes its bar through `action_bar::with_help(palette, bar, hint)`, which adds `F1 help` just before the closing run of Esc and `q` hints (`… F1 help  Esc back`), or last when the bar opens with Esc, and puts the screen's hint key just before it: `Hint::Question` shows `? hint`, `Hint::ShiftH` shows `H hint` (Activity, Playstyle and the Commander maybeboard, whose `?` explains the focused section), and `Hint::Absent` shows none. Any hint a bar names itself is dropped, so it shows once under one label. A screen passes `Absent` while a field takes the letters, since `?` then types (Import on its text box, the deck form on Name, Price and the card fields and in its pickers, the dictionary and the maybeboard while typing). The sign-in screens have no hint and list `F1 help` themselves, in the bars Screens describes. F1 is caught in the event loop before any screen, so it opens `help`, the Help & feedback dialog (`help::open(cx)`), everywhere, even while a text field takes letters; `!` still opens it wherever no field takes letters.
- `skeleton`: ghost rows for content still loading.
- `browser`: `browser::open(cx, url)` runs `open` on macOS and `xdg-open` elsewhere, and shows the link in a toast when that fails.
- `clipboard`: `clipboard::copy(text)` writes an OSC 52 sequence.
- `mana`, `charts`, `panel`, `measure`: mana colors and pips, bar charts, a centered framed panel, and text sizes. `panel::bordered(palette)` is the frame of every box and fills it with the theme's background, so a box sits solid on the grid.

A widget no screen uses yet carries `expect(dead_code)` on its `mod` line in `widgets/mod.rs`, and an item no screen uses yet in a widget that is otherwise in use carries one of its own. The screen that starts using it deletes that line; the build says so if it is forgotten. A helper only tests call is `#[cfg(test)]`.
