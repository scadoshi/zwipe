# 1.21.9 release notes

One text, both stores. 474 characters, 26 to spare.

```
- Printings flips or melds every printing at once, with prices as one row
- Every flip turns a card's image and details together, with a fade
- Swipe cards wait for their image and load as a card-shaped ghost
- Undoing a swipe up brings the card back from the top
- A deck's total updates as soon as you change a printing
- Settings and Account use a pencil and a sun or moon
- Clearer shadows, field borders and spacing
- No stuck highlight after a tap
- Many smaller fixes
```

The phone-visible entries from 1.21.5 through 1.21.9 in `zwipe-core/src/content/changelog/mod.rs`, the first store build since 1.21.4. Web-only changes (keyboard, the web swipe screen, menus) fall into the last line; the in-app changelog carries all of them.
