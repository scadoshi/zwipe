# 8. Pick your commander

**Status:** Planned.

**Theme:** Choosing a commander by swiping, with its partner filled in for you and Synergy ready the moment it's picked.

## Soundtrack

Epic and regal: a crowning brass theme, timpani, sweeping strings, about 110 bpm.

## Script

Callum, the mix delivery (see README). Hook on screen: *Swipe for your Magic: The Gathering commander*

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | [dramatically] Swipe for your Magic commander. |
| 2 | The most played, first. |
| 3 | Pick a partner commander and its partner fills in. |
| 4 | Synergy starts with cards that fit it. |
| 5 | [shouts] Crown your commander! [serious] Then build around it. |
| End card | Zwipe. The Magic: The Gathering deck builder. Free, no ads, on iOS, Android and web. |

## Pacing

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Create deck, Swipe beside Commander |
| 3-6s | Swipe select: a few left, popular faces |
| 6-9s | Pick Pir, Toothy fills in on its own |
| 9-13s | Add with Synergy on: the first cards fit the commander |
| 13-16s | A burst of swipes |
| 16-23s | End card, closing line |
