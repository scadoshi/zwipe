# 1.21.4 release notes

One text, both stores. 493 characters, 7 to spare.

```
- A deck opens on its Profile, with Cards in the dock
- A deck's cards show as a List or as Stacks
- Each card group has a price and a cart for TCGplayer or Card Kingdom
- Card details close on a tap outside, the x or a drag
- Printings shows each printing's set, number and artist
- Quick add offers the most played cards first, in their cheapest printing
- Undo puts back stars and a warning's Remove or Fix to
- Sheets show a drag handle; card actions are bigger to tap
- Many smaller fixes
```

The phone-visible entries from 1.19.0 through 1.21.4 in `zwipe-core/src/content/changelog/mod.rs`, the first store build since 1.18.15. The web app, the keyboard shortcuts, zwipe.net and the screen reader work fall into the last line; the in-app changelog carries all of them.
