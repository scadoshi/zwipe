# 7. Share your deck

**Status:** Planned.

**Theme:** A deck as a link: it unfurls into a preview card wherever it's pasted, and opens a page that leads with the deck's MVPs.

## Soundtrack

Upbeat and bright: hand percussion, plucky strings, a confident brass hook, about 120 bpm.

## Script

Callum, the mix delivery (see README). Hook on screen: *Your Magic: The Gathering deck, one link*

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | [dramatically] Your Magic deck. One link. |
| 2 | Star your three MVPs. |
| 3 | Paste it anywhere and it shows. |
| 4 | Anyone can open it, no account. |
| 5 | [shouts] Show it off! [serious] Then keep building. |
| End card | Zwipe. The Magic: The Gathering deck builder. Free, no ads, on iOS, Android and web. |

## Pacing

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Deck view, tap Share, the link copies |
| 3-6s | Star three cards as MVPs |
| 6-9s | The link pasted into a chat, the preview card unfurls |
| 9-13s | zwipe.net/deck page, MVPs at the top |
| 13-16s | Back to the app, swipe a card in |
| 16-23s | End card, closing line |
