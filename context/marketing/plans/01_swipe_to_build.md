# 1. Swipe to build

**Status:** Done (v3, 2026-11-08). Spec and renders in ~/Developer/video-factory/videos/swipe-to-build/.

**Theme:** The core loop: swipe right to add, left to skip, with the filter, Synergy and the hints in passing.

## Soundtrack

Epic orchestral boss battle: war drums, taiko, brass fanfares over racing low strings, building the whole way (ElevenLabs track A).

## Script

Callum, the mix delivery (see README). Hook on screen: *Swipe to build*

| # | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | [dramatically] Swipe right to add. Left to skip! |
| 2 | Filter down to the types you want. |
| 3 | Turn on Synergy for cards that fit your commander. |
| 4 | Stuck? Every screen has a hint. |
| 5 | [shouts] Gather your cards! Build your army! [serious] One swipe at a time. |
| End card | Zwipe. The Magic: The Gathering deck builder. Free, no ads, on iOS, Android and web. |

## Pacing

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Rapid swiping on Add, cards flying left and right |
| 3-5s | Filter sheet: Types, Creature and Artifact picked |
| 5-8s | Synergy chip off to on, the pile redeals |
| 8-11s | The Swipe to build hint opens and closes |
| 11-16s | A last burst of swipes |
| 16-24s | End card, closing line |
